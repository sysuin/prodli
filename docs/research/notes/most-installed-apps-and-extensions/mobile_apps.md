# Most downloaded mobile apps 2025–2026 (productivity, utilities, AI, education, habits) and what ProdLi could bring in

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. Download figures are third-party estimates (AppTweak, Appfigures, Sensor Tower, AppMagic). They use different methods and often disagree; each figure is attributed to its source. No 2026 full-year rankings exist yet; 2026 data is monthly or YTD.

## 1. Top downloaded apps overall (2025 and 2026 YTD), and which productivity/AI apps make the lists

### Takeaway
ChatGPT was the world's most downloaded app of 2025 by AppTweak's count (845M), and it was still #1 by month in August 2026. AI assistants are now the only "productivity" apps in the overall top lists: Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok and Copilot lead the AI and productivity rankings. Claude moved from absent in 2025 to a top-3 App Store download and top-10 revenue app in 2026.

## 3. Which mobile features drive downloads and retention

### Takeaway
The best public evidence is Duolingo's: streaks and streak widgets correlate with much better retention, and A/B tests on streak mechanics show measurable lifts in Day-7 and Day-14 retention. AI chat is the single biggest download driver of 2025–26. Short-form, gamified learning (Seekho) and AI-powered note and planner apps were what the stores' editors highlighted.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo says learners who use its widget "had far better retention" even after controlling for self-selection, and half of learners with the widget have a streak of 6 months or more. The widget includes an iOS lock-screen version designed to prompt a tap that extends the streak — [Duolingo blog, widget feature](https://blog.duolingo.com/widget-feature). This is correlational company data with no published sample size or method.
- In a Duolingo A/B test, separating the daily goal from the streak raised Day-14 retention by 3.3% (relative). A streak-wager test raised Day-1, Day-7 and Day-14 retention, with Day-7 up 14% — [Duolingo blog, improving the streak](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak); [Duolingo engineering blog](https://making.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals)
- Sensor Tower: in the month after Busuu added a streak feature, total sessions rose 15% (older analysis, pre-2025) — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/duolingo-streak-feature-app-engagement-growth)
- New image models and free premium AI plans drove AI-assistant download growth in 2025 (e.g. India GenAI downloads tripled) — [TechCrunch India](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/indias-app-downloads-rebounded-to-25-5-billion-in-2025-fueled-by-ai-assistants-and-microdrama-boom/)
- Editors' picks emphasise AI note-taking (Goodnotes), task lists combined with journaling and mood (Daily Planner), wearables (sleep app on watch), and gamified bite-size learning (Seekho) — [Google Play Best of 2025 India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/google-play-best-apps-and-games-of-2025/)

### Inferences
- Streaks and widgets are the best-evidenced retention levers for a daily-use app. ProdLi's habits feature (check/count/timed/quit) is a natural host for a streak plus a lock-screen or home-screen widget.
- Share-sheet capture, document scanning with OCR, voice memo transcription, offline use and Live Activities are widely used patterns, but I found no 2025–26 public data that isolates their effect on retention.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced data on Live Activities, the Dynamic Island, share-sheet extensions, offline mode or Apple Watch/Wear OS companions as download or retention drivers.

## 4. India specifics

### Takeaway
India was the world's largest download market at 25.5B in 2025, but spends little: IAP revenue just passed $1B. Utilities and productivity make up over a third of installs. GenAI downloads tripled to 602M, driven by free-for-a-year premium AI offers from OpenAI, Google with Jio, and Perplexity with Airtel. Price sensitivity is extreme, and the standard local price anchor is ₹399/month (ChatGPT Go).

### Cited Findings
- India's downloads rebounded to 25.5B in 2025 (24.6B in 2024, 25.9B in 2023). Time spent grew to 1.23T hours. India is not in the top 20 markets by consumer spending — [TechCrunch, 21 Jan 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/indias-app-downloads-rebounded-to-25-5-billion-in-2025-fueled-by-ai-assistants-and-microdrama-boom/)
- India's GenAI app downloads rose from 198M (2024) to 602M (2025). AI-assistant downloads grew by 346M and short-drama apps by more than 350M. ChatGPT was the top GenAI app, followed by Gemini, Perplexity and Grok, and ranked just behind Instagram in overall downloads — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/indias-app-downloads-rebounded-to-25-5-billion-in-2025-fueled-by-ai-assistants-and-microdrama-boom/)
- Utilities and Productivity together made up over one-third of India's installs in 2025. IAP revenue passed $1B in 2025 and is projected at $1.25B by end of 2026. Jobs & Education is a faster-growing category — [Sensor Tower, State of India 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-india-mobile-app-market-2026)
- Apple named ChatGPT the top free iPhone app in India for 2025 — [News Karnataka](https://newskarnataka.com/business/apple-reveals-top-10-most-downloaded-free-apps-in-india-for-2025/14122025/) (secondary source)
- In August 2025, India's free Productivity charts were led by AI chatbots (Perplexity #1 on iOS, helped by the Airtel offer, plus ChatGPT), telecom utilities (MyJio) and public-service apps — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/top-5-free-productivity-apps-in-india-perplexity-chatgpt-myjio-dominate-charts-on-google-play-and-app-store)
- Pricing and free offers: ChatGPT Go launched at ₹399/month including GST and was made free for a year for Indian users from 4 Nov 2025. Jio users got 18 months of Google AI Pro, valued at about ₹35,100. Airtel bundled Perplexity Pro for a year, worth about ₹17,000 (list price about ₹1,417/month). Perplexity reportedly cannot yet take rupees for recurring plans. One source says the claim windows for ChatGPT Go and Airtel–Perplexity closed in mid/late January 2026 — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/how-founders-use-ai/choosing-an-assistant/what-do-these-ai-plans-cost-in-india-and-are-the-free-jio-and-airtel-offers-still-running); [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/apps/chatgpt-gemini-and-perplexity-pro-paid-subscriptions-are-now-free-in-india-heres-how-to-get-them.html); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=528940)
- Google Play's weekly India top-free chart included ChatGPT, Kuku TV, RailOne, Seekho (short learning videos) and Meesho — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/technology/google-play-store-top-free-apps-list-chatgpt-kuku-tv-railone-seekho-and-meesho-among-most-downloaded-play-store-apps-this-week-6975321.html)
- Google Play India Best of 2025 favoured India-specific content and vernacular/local experiences (District, Toonsutra with Indian creators, Real Cricket) — [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/google-play-best-apps-and-games-of-2025/)

### Inferences
- In India, a ProdLi paid tier has to compete with AI plans that were recently free or cost ₹399/month. A low-cost plan (₹99–199/month or a yearly plan) with UPI Autopay through Google Play Billing is likely needed. A generous free tier with a habit streak is the acquisition path.
- Hindi and Indian-language support for voice dictation, meeting transcription and the AI brief could set ProdLi apart, since Indian downloads lean toward local content.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced 2025 ranking of India's most downloaded productivity apps (e.g. WPS Office, CamScanner, Truecaller, DigiLocker) or education apps (e.g. PW, Seekho, Duolingo India). No data on UPI-subscription conversion rates or vernacular-usage share for productivity apps turned up either.

## 5. Which features fit ProdLi's phone app and web app (ranked by fit and effort), and which are a bad fit

### Takeaway
The strongest additions are mobile capture surfaces that feed ProdLi's existing to-dos, notes, habits and Ask AI: home-screen and lock-screen widgets with habit streaks, share-sheet capture, voice memo → tasks/notes, and document scanning → notes with OCR. Avoid building a general AI chatbot, a PDF editor or office suite, or a file manager, since the leaders own those categories.

### Cited Findings
- Streak widgets correlate with stronger retention (Duolingo), and streak mechanics show A/B-tested retention lifts — [Duolingo widget](https://blog.duolingo.com/widget-feature); [Duolingo streak](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)
- PDF and scanner utilities and AI assistants dominate Productivity downloads — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/reports/most-downloaded-productivity-tools-apps?format=md)
- Editors highlighted task lists combined with journaling and mood (Daily Planner) and AI notes (Goodnotes) — [Google Play India 2025](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/google-play-best-apps-and-games-of-2025/)
- AI apps with paid tiers earn well per download (Claude $5.92, ChatGPT $4.29 in August 2026) — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/most-downloaded-highest-earning-apps-august-2026)

Bad fit (not worth building):
- A general AI chatbot competing with ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. Keep Ask AI scoped to the user's own data.
- A full PDF editor, office suite or file manager (Acrobat, WPS, Microsoft 365).
- Authenticator or password manager: trust and security burden, off-mission.
- Short-video learning or microdrama content.
- QR scanner as a standalone feature: the OS camera already does it, so add value only as a by-product of the scanner.
