# Most-Used Features of Leading All-in-One Workspace and Note-Taking Apps (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-18. Budget-limited pass (about 15 search/fetch calls). Several primary pages failed to load: the State of Obsidian 2026 survey domain did not resolve, and the Business Wire Evernote v11 release returned a 403. Many hits were SEO comparison or "statistics" sites. Those are flagged where used, and low-quality numbers were left out.

## For each app: user numbers or rank (2025–2026), core features, and the features users rely on most

### Takeaway
Notion is by far the largest of the "workspace" apps: 100M+ users, 4M+ paying customers, and roughly $500M+ annualized revenue (2025). Most of the others either publish no user numbers (Obsidian, Craft, Capacities, Heptabase, Anytype, Logseq, Mem, Reflect) or are bundled OS/suite apps (Apple Notes, Google Keep, OneNote) that don't break out usage. The hardest *actual-usage* evidence available is (a) Chrome Web Store install counts for web clippers, (b) Obsidian plugin download counts, (c) Notion's AI-payer share and agent-creation numbers, and (d) Evernote's statement that usage limits drove 51% of its 2025 subscription conversions.

### Inferences
- **Web clipping is a real, used feature.** Evernote (2M), OneNote (1M) and Notion (1M) all have seven-figure clipper installs. Notion's clipper is rated poorly (about 3/5), which points to an opening for a better clipper inside a Chrome extension.
- **Templates and database/table views are the features power users actively install.** Two of Obsidian's top three plugins (Templater, Dataview) add them, and Obsidian built Bases (database views) into core.
- **Visual/whiteboard thinking is in heavy demand.** Excalidraw is Obsidian's #1 plugin, and canvas-style tools (Obsidian Canvas, Heptabase) are differentiators.
- **Hitting limits converts free users.** Evernote's 51% figure suggests caps on notes, devices or AI work as freemium levers.
- **Smaller PKM apps are unstable choices.** Logseq's split, the Coda rebrand and Loop's component retirements mean users of these tools are churning or re-evaluating.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 user counts were found for Obsidian, Evernote MAU, OneNote, Apple Notes, Google Keep, Craft, Capacities, Heptabase, Anytype, Logseq, Mem or Reflect.
- The State of Obsidian 2026 survey results (feature-usage percentages) could not be retrieved because the domain did not resolve. This would be the best single usage dataset; worth checking manually.
- Nothing usable came back on Craft, Capacities or Mem (including "Mem 2.0") for 2025–2026.
- No verified Google Keep Chrome Web Store count, and no Play Store download figures.
- No app-store ranking data (iOS/Android top-chart positions) was retrieved.

## What AI features shipped in 2025–2026, and which ones do users actually adopt?

### Takeaway
AI shipped everywhere: meeting notes/transcription, semantic/enterprise search, AI writing, and (in Notion) autonomous agents. The only hard adoption numbers are Notion's. Over 50% of Notion customers now pay for AI (up from 10–20% the year before), and users built 1M+ Custom Agents in the two months after launch. Elsewhere, adoption is unmeasured, and vendors are moving AI behind paywalls or credits.

### Cited Findings
- **Notion 2.51 (May 13, 2025):** AI Meeting Notes (transcription, summaries, action items pushed into task databases), Enterprise Search across connected apps, and the separate AI add-on folded into plans (May 2025). — [Notion 2.51 release](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/releases/2025-05-13); [Taskade history](https://www.taskade.com/blog/notion-ai-history) (secondary)
- **Notion 3.0 (Sept 18, 2025):** AI Agents that take multi-step actions and have memory. — [Notion 3.0 release](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/releases/2025-09-18)
- **Notion 3.3 (Feb 24, 2026):** Custom Agents that connect to Slack, Mail, Calendar and MCP tools, with every run logged and reversible. Since May 4, 2026 they run on purchasable "Notion Credits" (free trial until May 3). More connectors have been added (Mercury, Mixpanel, Miro, Box, ClickHouse), plus audit logs on Enterprise. — [Notion 3.3 release](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24); [Notion release 2026-04-14](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-04-14)
- **Adoption (Notion):** Users built **more than 1 million AI agents within two months** of Custom Agents launching. — [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/notion-ai-agents-for-a-workspace-used-by-100-million-people)
- **Adoption (Notion):** The COO said customers paying for AI went from 10–20% the year before, to 30–40%, to "recently crossed 50%" (2025). Sacra says over 50% of ARR came from AI-enabled customers at the end of 2025, more than double the prior year. — [newsio (CNBC syndication)](https://newsio.com/2025/09/18/notion-rides-ai-boom-to-500-million-in-annual-revenue-but-microsoft-competition-looms/?amp=1); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/notion)
- **Notion 3.4–3.6 (2026):** Meeting Notes gained custom summary instructions, speaker identification and audio-file upload. Notion AI can now schedule meetings and draft emails through Mail & Calendar, and there's an Outlook Mail/Calendar connection. — [Notion 3.6 release](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-07-01); [Notion release 2026-04-14](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-04-14)
- Notion is also reported to have opened a developer platform for outside agents, bought an AI model startup, and started charging for work done rather than seats. — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/to-scale-agentic-ai-notion-tore-down-its-tech-stack-and-started-fresh); [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/notion-ai-agents-for-a-workspace-used-by-100-million-people)
- **Evernote v11 (Jan 2026):** AI Assistant, Semantic Search and AI Meeting Notes, plus AI Transcribe and AI Rewrite. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260119103272/en) (snippet); [Flozic](https://www.flozic.ai/blog/reviews/evernote-review)
- **OneNote (2026):** Copilot Notebooks on web and iOS. Multimodal capture (audio + photo + typing) becomes a structured page. In-app AI now needs a paid Copilot license. — [Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes); [Templacity](https://templacity.com/onenote/copilot-in-onenote/)
- **Loop:** Copilot Recaps are reportedly being removed, which suggests weak uptake of some AI summary features. — [Windows Report Loop tag](https://windowsreport.com/tag/microsoft-loop/) (headline only; year unconfirmed)
- **Apple Notes:** audio recording with transcription and AI summaries (iOS 18). — [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/new-features-apple-notes-app-ios-18)
- **Google:** Gemini in Keep on paid Workspace plans. Gemini app notebooks synced with NotebookLM (free tier: 50 sources per notebook). — [Atlas Workspace](https://www.atlasworkspace.ai/blog/onenote-vs-google-keep); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/04/30/gemini-notebooks-android-ios/)
- **Grammarly → Superhuman (Oct 2025):** launched the "Superhuman Go" AI assistant across the suite, including Coda/Superhuman Docs. — [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/grammarly-rebrands-as-superhuman-introduces-superhuman-go-20251030)

### Inferences
- **The AI features that converge across vendors are:** (1) meeting/voice transcription with summary and action items (Notion, Evernote, OneNote, Apple Notes), (2) semantic or enterprise search / Q&A over your own notes (Notion, Evernote, OneNote Copilot Notebooks, Gemini/NotebookLM), and (3) AI rewrite/writing help. These are becoming table stakes in 2026.
- **Agents are Notion's differentiator.** Its 1M-agents figure is the only hard adoption signal. "Agents built" is not the same as "agents in regular use," though.
- **The money is moving to usage-based AI** (Notion credits, Copilot license gating). A small extension team should expect AI cost to need a usage cap or paid tier.

### Gaps
- No usage or retention numbers for Notion AI Meeting Notes or Enterprise Search specifically. One stats site claimed an "expanding majority of paid teams" and was excluded as unreliable.
- No adoption data for Evernote, OneNote, Apple or Google AI features.

## What do users most often praise, complain about, or request?

### Takeaway
The complaints repeat across apps. Speed and slow quick capture (especially Notion on mobile), weak offline mode, sync failures and data loss, poor search in large workspaces, and price increases or paywalls (especially Evernote). Users praise flexibility (Notion databases and templates), local-first ownership and plugins (Obsidian), and fast, frictionless capture (Keep, Apple Notes). No rigorous 2025–2026 cross-app user survey was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Notion is slow, especially on mobile.** Reviewers cite laggy typing, slow loads and mobile sync problems, and say a quick note on the move is "very hard" because of load times. — [Fibery Openion: Notion](https://fibery.io/openion/notion-2); [Fibery: Android performance](https://fibery.io/openion/notion-2/android-text-input-and-performance-issues-243937)
- **Notion slows down in large workspaces.** A 2025 reviewer said it feels "slow or laggy" when switching between large pages and databases. — [AWS Marketplace reviews](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/reviews/reviews-list/prodview-eoc4gcvwcs3a6?page=175); [thebusinessdive review](https://thebusinessdive.com/notion-review)
- **Notion's offline mode (Aug 2025) is limited.** Only 50 database rows per view are available offline, AI needs a connection, and offline edits sync later. — [21notion](https://21notion.com/en/blog/notion-offline-mode-review-2025)
- **Notion search misses content.** It misses text in toggles and synced blocks, and gets harder with many notes. — [2sync](https://2sync.com/blog/is-notion-a-good-note-taking-app); [tryorbye](https://www.tryorbye.com/products/notion)
- **Across note apps, sync failures and data loss are the top complaint (22% of low-star reviews).** The same analysis says local-first apps avoid about 60% of top complaints. This is a single vendor-blog analysis with unclear method. — [unstar.app ranking](https://unstar.app/blog/notion-evernote-obsidian-apple-notes-onenote-note-taking-apps-ranked-2026)
- **Notion churn intent (older data, late 2023):** over 40% of surveyed Notion users said they were testing other tools. — cited in [venturz](https://venturz.co/comparisons/notion-alternatives) (dated, secondary)
- **Evernote pricing backlash.** Users felt the price rises weren't justified by "killer features," and some reported prices doubling in a year. — [Pragmatic Engineer](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-bending-spoons-acquisition-strategy/); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/evernote-pricing)
- **Evernote's free plan feels "effectively paywalled"** (50 notes, 1 device). — [Flozic](https://www.flozic.ai/blog/reviews/evernote-review)
- **Logseq users are leaving** over slow development and the risky DB migration. — [dessence](https://dessence.ai/blog/logseq-alternatives-after-stalled-development-2026)
- **Web clipper ratings show a quality gap.** OneNote clipper 4.8 (68K ratings) and Evernote 4.5–4.7 vs Notion 2.9–3.3. — [Chrome Web Store: OneNote](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/onenote-web-clipper/gojbdfnpnhogfdgjbigejoaolejmgdhk?hl=en); [Chrome Web Store: Notion](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/notion-web-clipper/knheggckgoiihginacbkhaalnibhilkk?hl=en)
- **Microsoft is cutting cross-app embedding** (Planner in Loop, Loop in OneNote web). — [Microsoft Tech Community](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft_365blog/copilot-pages--notebooks-microsoft-loop-it-admin-update-%E2%80%93-december-2025/4474306); [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/tag/microsoft-loop/)

### Inferences
- **The most common unmet need is fast, reliable capture that works offline.** A Chrome extension already lives in the browser, so it can win on instant capture (side panel, hotkey, local-first storage) where Notion is weakest.
- **Search quality and sync reliability matter more than feature count.** Data loss is the top category of 1-star reviews.
- **Price sensitivity is high** after Evernote. A generous free core with clear limits is safer than aggressive caps.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Reddit-wide or formal survey with ranked feature requests was found. Evidence is reviewer- and blog-level.
- No G2/Capterra aggregate scores were retrieved this pass.

## Which features recur across most apps ("table stakes") vs. differentiators?

### Takeaway
Table stakes in 2026: rich-text notes with checklists, search, tags or folders, cross-device sync, sharing, templates, web clipping, mobile/quick capture, and now basic AI (summarise, rewrite, transcribe). Differentiators: databases with multiple views (Notion, Obsidian Bases, Coda, Anytype, Capacities), backlinks/graph and daily notes (Obsidian, Logseq, Reflect, Capacities), canvas/whiteboards (Obsidian Canvas/Excalidraw, Heptabase), local-first and E2E privacy (Obsidian, Anytype, Logseq), autonomous agents with app connectors (Notion), and math/handwriting (Apple Notes).

### Cited Findings
- **Obsidian's own survey treats these as core features:** backlinks, Bases, Canvas, command palette, daily notes, graph view, Properties, search, tags, templates, Web Clipper. — [State of Obsidian 2026 Survey](https://2026.obsidian.md/survey/usage)
- **Top Obsidian plugins add** drawing/canvas (Excalidraw), database queries (Dataview), templates (Templater), tasks (Tasks), version control (Git), tables (Advanced Tables) and calendar/daily-note navigation (Calendar). — [ObsidianStats](https://www.obsidianstats.com/most-downloaded); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/best-obsidian-plugins/)
- **Notion's AI Meeting Notes push action items into task databases**, linking AI capture to structured data. — [Taskade history](https://www.taskade.com/blog/notion-ai-history)
- **Meeting notes / transcription are now in Notion, Evernote, OneNote (Copilot) and Apple Notes.** — [Notion 2.51](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/releases/2025-05-13); [Flozic](https://www.flozic.ai/blog/reviews/evernote-review); [Templacity](https://templacity.com/onenote/copilot-in-onenote/); [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/new-features-apple-notes-app-ios-18)
- **Web clipper extensions exist for Notion, Evernote and OneNote**, each with 1M+ Chrome users. — [Chrome Web Store: Evernote](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/evernote-web-clipper/pioclpoplcdbaefihamjohnefbikjilc); [Chrome Web Store: OneNote](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/onenote-web-clipper/gojbdfnpnhogfdgjbigejoaolejmgdhk?hl=en); [Chrome Web Store: Notion](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/notion-web-clipper/knheggckgoiihginacbkhaalnibhilkk?hl=en)
- **Local-first plus E2E encryption is Anytype's main pitch.** Logseq and Reflect Open use plain Markdown files. — [OpenAlternative](https://openalternative.co/compare/anytype/vs/logseq); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/logseq-alternatives/)
- **Apple Notes' Math Notes and handwriting are platform-specific differentiators** (iOS 18). — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-notes-app/)
- **Offline mode is now expected.** Notion added it only in Aug 2025, after long user demand. — [21notion](https://21notion.com/en/blog/notion-offline-mode-review-2025)

### Inferences
Suggested prioritisation for a small team's all-in-one Chrome extension, based on usage signals above:
1. **Must-have (table stakes, strong usage evidence):** quick capture notes with checklists, fast full-text search, tags, a web clipper or highlighter (proven 1–2M install demand; incumbent Notion clipper rated poorly), templates (Templater about 5.3M downloads), and local storage plus sync with offline support (top complaint area).
2. **High-value next:** a lightweight database or table/kanban view of notes and tasks (Dataview about 5.3M, Bases, Notion's core), daily notes with calendar navigation, and reminders/tasks.
3. **AI that is converging into table stakes:** summarise a page or clip, rewrite, and Q&A over saved notes (semantic search). Gate it behind usage caps, as incumbents do.
4. **Differentiators to consider later:** backlinks/graph (power-user niche), canvas/whiteboard (Excalidraw is #1 on Obsidian, but heavy to build), meeting transcription (crowded: Notion, Evernote, OneNote, Apple), and agents with connectors (Notion's turf, expensive).
- These rankings mix hard proxies (installs, downloads, Notion AI payer share) with reviewer anecdotes. Only the former count as usage evidence.

### Gaps
- No cross-app quantitative ranking of feature usage exists in the sources found. The table-stakes list is inferred from feature overlap plus the proxies above.
- No data on backlinks/graph view usage rates. The graph view is widely advertised, but its real usage is unknown (the Obsidian survey results would answer this).
- No data on how often reminders, OCR or publishing features are used in any app.
